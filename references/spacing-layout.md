# Spacing & layout

Space is the cheapest way to look expensive. Rhythm and alignment separate designed from generated.

## Spacing
- Use one consistent scale (4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 128). Don't freehand pixel values.
- **Generous section spacing.** Cramped vertical rhythm reads as a template. Big, confident gaps between
  major sections (96–160px on desktop) signal intent. Measured example: Linear's landing runs a 128px
  section rhythm.
- Group by proximity: related things close, unrelated things far. Whitespace is structure, not filler.

## Grid & alignment
- Pick a grid and hold it. Everything aligns to it — ragged left edges are the most common slop tell.
- **Asymmetry beats dead-center.** An off-center hero, a 60/40 split, a left rail + fluid content reads
  more considered than perfectly mirrored columns.
- Bento grids: vary tile sizes with intent (one hero tile + supporting). Equal tiles = boring.

## Density
- Match density to the job: marketing pages breathe; dashboards/tables are dense but aligned.
- Orchestrate it across the page — a loud spacious hero, then a denser proof section, then quiet footer.
  Same rhythm everywhere is monotonous.

## Containers
- Cap content width (≈1200–1280px) and keep comfortable side gutters. Full-bleed only for deliberate moments.
- Don't stack three nested cards (card-in-card-in-card). Flatten; use one surface language.
